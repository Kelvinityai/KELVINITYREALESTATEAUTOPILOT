Who We Are

Digital skills and solutions for a changing world.

Excellent Computer Business is an ICT training and digital solutions business focused on helping people develop useful technology skills and helping organisations make better use of digital tools.

Led by Ajagba Kelvin, also known as Kelvinity, our work spans computer training, programming, artificial intelligence, website and mobile app development, computer repairs, computer sales, graphic design, digital marketing, and video production.

Our mission is to make technology and digital creativity more accessible through practical training, professional services, and supportive guidance.
